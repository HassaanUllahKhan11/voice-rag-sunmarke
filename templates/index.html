How it works

  1. It reads your site

    Every public page, politely and within your robots.txt rules. Contact details are pulled out so they’re always findable.

  2. It finds the right passage

    Each question is matched to the most relevant parts of your pages, in milliseconds.

  3. It answers from those words only

    An AI model writes a short, friendly answer from those passages and cites the page it came from.
